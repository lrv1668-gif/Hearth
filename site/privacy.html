Privacy Policy.

Hearth is software you run on your own computer. Your data stays on that computer and the services you connect it to. The developer of Hearth never receives any of it.

Who this covers

This policy covers the Hearth home dashboard and its e-paper frame (“Hearth”), including the Google sign-in that Hearth uses to show your Google Calendar events and Google Tasks. Hearth is open source and is published by Lucas Vertrees (“we”). We don't run a hosted version of Hearth or any servers your Hearth talks to.

Where your data lives

Each Hearth install runs on a machine its owner controls, called the host. Everything Hearth stores is kept in databases on that host, such as tasks, settings, cached calendar items and sign-in tokens. Hearth contacts third-party services directly from the host. Nothing is sent to us, and Hearth has no analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking.

Google user data

When you connect a Google account, Hearth asks for two permissions:

  • Google Calendar, read-only (calendar.readonly). Hearth reads your events from yesterday through the next 60 days to show them on the dashboard, the calendar page and the frame. It never creates, changes or deletes events.
  • Google Tasks (tasks). Hearth reads your task lists and tasks to show them. It changes a task only when you tick or untick its checkbox in Hearth, which marks that task complete or not complete.

How it's stored. Google's access and refresh tokens are encrypted and stored in the calendar service's database on your host. Events and tasks are cached there for a few minutes so the screen doesn't ask Google on every load. All of this stays on your host.

How it's shared. Hearth doesn't share, sell or transfer Google user data to anyone. It only shows that data on the screens and frame of the Hearth install that you signed in to. Google user data is never used to serve ads, and never used to develop, improve or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Hearth's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services you connect

Depending on what you set up, your host may contact other services, for example Spotify (what's playing), Open-Meteo (weather for your location), Unsplash (photos), eBird (nearby birds), Transitland (departures), ESPN (scores), Wikipedia, ZenQuotes, the RSS feeds you add and any calendars you subscribe to by link (such as an Apple Calendar public link, which Hearth stores encrypted). Those requests go straight from your host to the service, and each service's own privacy policy applies. Some requests include the location you set in Hearth, so the service can return local weather, sightings or departures.

Deleting your data

  • Disconnect Google in Hearth's Settings → Connections. This deletes the stored tokens and the cached calendar items from your host.
  • Revoke access at any time from your Google Account's third-party connections page.
  • Remove everything by deleting Hearth's Docker volumes on your host.

Children

Hearth isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect anyone's data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Earlier versions are in the project's history on GitHub.
